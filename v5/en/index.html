What is a certified translation?

It is a translation completed and attested by a translator who is a member of the Ordre des traducteurs, terminologues et interprètes agréés du Québec (OTTIAQ). It is often required by government bodies, schools and professional orders.

How much does a translation cost?

The price depends on the volume, the type of document and the turnaround you need. Send your document to receive an accurate quote, with no obligation.

How long does it take?

It depends on the length and complexity of the text. The delivery date is stated in the quote; urgent requests can be considered.

How should I send my documents?

A PDF or Word file works, as does a clear scan or photo of your document.

Are my documents kept confidential?

Yes. Your documents and their content are handled in strict confidence and are never shared with third parties.

Do you also translate from English into French?

My language pair is French into English — that is the direction in which I guarantee the best quality.
